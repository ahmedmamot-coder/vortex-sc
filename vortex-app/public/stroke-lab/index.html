Mark events

Pause on the exact frame, then tap the event. Use the frame buttons for 1/30 s precision. Tap a set marker again to move it to the current frame.

Start & turn splits

PhaseFrom → toTime

5 m / 15 m marks are timed when the swimmer's head crosses the lane-rope colour change. Film starts from the side at 90° to the lane for accurate timing.

Set the scale

Velocity and distance per cycle need metres, not pixels. Tap two points on the video that are a known distance apart (for example two lane-rope colour changes, or the wall and the 5 m mark), then enter that distance.

Demo scale: frame width = 8 m
