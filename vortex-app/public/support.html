Support

We can help

Vortex SC is the members’ app for Vortex Swimming Club, training at Hamad Aquatic Center, Doha. If something is not working, or you need something changed, here is where to go.

Contact us

  • App problems, accounts and sign-in: support@vortexswimmingclub.com
  • Privacy, your data, deleting an account: privacy@vortexswimmingclub.com
  • Reporting content, or a concern about a child: safeguarding@vortexswimmingclub.com
  • Coaching, squads and fees: speak to your child’s coach at the pool.

We answer support email within two working days. Reports of objectionable content are reviewed within 24 hours, and a safeguarding concern is escalated immediately.

Common questions

I cannot sign in

Use the same email address you registered with. If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we will email you a reset link. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then email support.

My child is not showing

A child is linked to your account when you register, and confirming their date of birth is what proves the link is yours to make. If your child’s date of birth is not on file, the app will say so — ask a coach to add it, then link again.

I want to delete my account

In the app: Settings → Your account → Delete my account. Your parent account, your contact details and the link to your children are deleted. Your child stays registered with the Club — attendance, results and training records are the Club’s own record and are kept, as described in the Privacy Policy.

I want to report a post or a message

Tap the ⋯ control on any post, comment or message and choose Report. You can also block a member so you no longer see their posts or receive their messages. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours.

Notifications are not arriving

Open Settings → Notifications in the app and turn them on. On iPhone, also check Settings → Vortex SC → Notifications on the device itself.
